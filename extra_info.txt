--- ABOUT THE INSTITUTION & HISTORY ---
![JACSICE Campus Main](https://www.jacsicoe.in/images/home_slider/collegephoto.jpg)
![JACSICE Campus View](https://www.jacsicoe.in/images/home_slider/college%20img%20sm.jpg)

Jayaraj Annapackiam CSI College of Engineering (JACSICE) was established in the millennium year 2001. It is situated in Nazareth, an elegant, semi-urban, and God-fearing town in Thoothukudi district. 
Motto: "Make Technology Reachable and Affordable to All".
Management: It is run by the CSI Thoothukudi - Nazareth Diocese, which proudly manages more than 350 educational institutions in the Thoothukudi District.
